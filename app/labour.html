Labour

A tight labour market, loosening slowly.

Unemployment averaged 4.0% in 2024, up from 3.6% in 2023 but still low by historical standards — below the 5.3% of 2015 and far below the 8.1% pandemic peak of 2020. About 175.4 million people were in the civilian labour force in 2024.

What households earn

Median household income, 2024

Real median household income was $83,730 in 2024, up 1.3% in real terms from $82,690 in 2023 (both in 2024 dollars). It was not statistically different from the 2023 estimate — income has essentially been flat in real terms since 2019, after growing for the first time since the pandemic in 2023.

Family households earned a median of $108,600; married-couple households $128,700; female householders with no spouse present $60,440.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Current Population Survey Annual Social and Economic Supplement, Current Population Reports P60-286 (September 2025).
