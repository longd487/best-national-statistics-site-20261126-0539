Economy

The largest economy in the world.

Nominal GDP reached $30.9 trillion in 2025. The economy grew 2.3% in real terms that year, after 3.0% in 2024 — the third consecutive year of growth above 2% following the 2020 contraction.

The public debt

Gross federal debt stood at $36.2 trillion at the end of December 2024, about 123% of GDP. Debt held by the public — the part financed in the bond market — was about $28.2 trillion.

General government gross debt was 120.8% of GDP in 2024 on the World Bank’s measure, which counts state and local government debt as well.

Source: U.S. Department of the Treasury, Fiscal Data; World Bank, World Development Indicators.

Reading the table

The 2020 dip is the pandemic recession: nominal GDP fell from $21.5 trillion to $21.4 trillion and real GDP contracted 2.2%. The rebound — +6.3% real growth in 2021 — was the fastest since 1984, and nominal GDP has risen every year since.
