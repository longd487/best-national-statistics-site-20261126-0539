Prices

Inflation is back near 3%.

Consumer prices rose 2.9% on average in 2024, down from the 8.0% peak of 2022 — the highest annual rate since 1981. Core inflation, which strips out food and energy, was 3.4% in 2024 and has fallen more slowly than the headline rate.
