Health, income and housing

Health

Life expectancy at birth was 78.4 years in 2023, up from 77.5 years in 2022 and 76.4 in 2021 — a second consecutive year of recovery after the pandemic, though still below the 78.8 years of 2019.

Source: National Center for Health Statistics, National Vital Statistics System.

Education

Mean years of schooling stood at 13.9 years in 2023. 95.5% of households have a computer and 91.0% a broadband internet subscription.

Sources: UNDP Human Development Report 2023–24; U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020–2024.

Income and poverty

Real median household income was $83,730 in 2024 (CPS ASEC). 10.2% of people lived below the poverty line on the American Community Survey’s 2020–2024 measure; the official poverty rate from the Current Population Survey was 11.1% in 2023.

The two poverty figures come from different surveys and are not directly comparable — see Methods.

Housing

There were 148.3 million housing units at 1 July 2025, and 65.2% of occupied units were owner-occupied (2020–2024).

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, QuickFacts (Population Estimates Program; American Community Survey).

A note on the numbers. Society figures come from several different surveys — the decennial census, the American Community Survey, the Current Population Survey and the vital statistics system — each with its own definitions. Figures that look similar can measure different things; the Methods page explains each one.
