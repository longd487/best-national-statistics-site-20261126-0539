Trade

The deficit widened to $918 billion.

In 2024 the United States exported $3,191.6 billion of goods and services and imported $4,110.0 billion — a deficit of $918.4 billion, 3.1% of GDP, up from $784.9 billion in 2023. Both exports and imports were the highest on record.

2024 in detail

Goods: exports $2,083.8 billion, imports $3,295.6 billion — a deficit of $1,211.7 billion, the highest on record.

Services: exports $1,107.4 billion, imports $814.4 billion — a surplus of $293.3 billion. Services exports were the highest on record.

Largest bilateral goods deficit: China, $295 billion.
